HOW NYRAVA HOME WORKS

A clear path from signal to follow-up.

Predictive insights start the research. Qualification determines whether an opportunity is ready for a Realtor conversation.

01 · PREDICTIVE INSIGHTS

Understand potential interest.

Predictive analytics help identify possible buying or selling interest in the areas you serve. Homeowner valuation, buying and selling tools also collect direct inquiries. A prediction, public listing or valuation request alone does not establish a qualified lead.

02 · LEAD QUALIFICATION

Separate signals from qualified leads.

Eligible delivery requires current buying or selling intent, confirmed contact details, and recorded permission to connect with the designated Realtor. Incomplete or uncertain prospects remain separate for screening or review. Matching also respects ZIP availability and subscription allowances.

03 · CONNECTED FOLLOW-UP

Keep the next step organized.

Work delivered opportunities in your Realtor workspace, assign follow-up to team members, and organize notes, tasks and appointments. AI can assist with summaries and message drafts. Your team chooses what to send. Availability, response and outcomes vary.

HOW WE DO IT

Build your business.
Keep your commission.

For Realtors, winning one listing can mean paying for multiple leads, investing hours in follow-up, and spending money on opportunities that never become clients. Some platforms also charge referral fees that take a substantial share of the commission when a transaction closes.

Nyrava Home was created to offer a more affordable approach: direct lead generation and the tools to manage those opportunities in one platform.

Our homeowner tools and advertising help attract people exploring their home’s value, planning to buy or sell, or looking for a Realtor in their area. Predictive analytics help identify potential interest, while contact verification, stated intent, and permission help determine which inquiries qualify for delivery.

Eligible inquiries are matched by ZIP code with participating licensed real estate professionals. Realtors and their teams can then organize contacts, manage assignments, track follow-ups, schedule appointments, and use AI assistance within the same workspace.

No Nyrava per-lead charges. No Nyrava commission-sharing fees at closing.

A $349 annual license includes the first service month. Monthly service begins in month two: Basic at $89/month or Pro at $149/month. The annual license renews yearly.

Our focus is qualified local opportunities and practical tools at a predictable platform price—so you can spend more time building relationships and helping clients move forward.

Lead availability varies by location and demand. Predictive signals do not prove intent, and lead volume, listings, and closings are not guaranteed.
