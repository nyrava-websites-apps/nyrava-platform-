HOME-VALUE REPORTS

Bring property context to the conversation.

Prepare home-value research from available comparable sales, with a clear distinction between professional reports and public homeowner requests.

For Realtors and their teams

Sign in to your authorized workspace and enter the property details, Realtor name, and client name. The professional tool does not require the client's contact information and does not create a consumer lead or use a lead allowance.

Reports are sent to the running user's saved work email when delivery is available. Keep your profile email current. Saved reports stay within authorized workspace access.

For homeowners

The public free valuation service requests name, email, phone, and property details. Those details are saved before the lookup. If an automated estimate is unavailable, the request remains available for admin review. Optional Realtor follow-up is separate from requesting a report.

When data is limited

An automated estimate depends on sufficiently relevant comparable sales. It may not be possible to prepare a range for every property. A report is not a formal appraisal or a guarantee of the price a buyer will pay.
