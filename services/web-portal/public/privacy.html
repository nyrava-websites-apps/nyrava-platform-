Privacy at Nyrava Home

Updated October 19, 2026.

Information you provide

Our buying and selling forms ask for contact details, a location, and information about your plans. A home-value request may include a property address and information needed to prepare an estimate. We keep submitted inquiries, verification and permission records, and service-related activity to operate the service.

How requests are handled

We use inquiry information to verify contact details, evaluate your request, and support the matching process. The inquiry form describes its contact permission and designated-agent authorization steps. Requesting an estimate alone does not authorize unrelated marketing.

Service providers

We use hosting, security, email and, where configured, messaging and valuation providers to operate these features. Public pages may be crawled by search engines. Keep confidential information out of public submissions and email.

Your questions and choices

Contact support@home.nyrava.com to ask about your information, correct a request, request deletion, or withdraw follow-up permission. Please identify the request without sending passwords or sensitive documents. Requests are reviewed with applicable recordkeeping needs.
