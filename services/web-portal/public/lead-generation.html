Where opportunities begin

Nyrava Home brings together homeowner inquiries submitted through its website and owner-sale prospects discovered by its collection tools. Public valuation requests are also captured for admin review, including requests where an automated report cannot be prepared.

What predictive insights mean

Available property signals can help identify possibilities worth reviewing. A property listing or signal does not establish that someone needs an agent, owns the property, or wants to be contacted. Review source information alongside what the person has actually shared.

A prospect and a qualified lead are different. A public contact detail alone is not verification or permission. Qualification considers current intent, contact details, and authorization to connect.

Focus on the communities you serve

Opportunities retain their actual property ZIP code. Eligible delivery depends on matching coverage, an active subscription, lead qualification, and plan limits. Checking ZIP availability does not reserve a subscriber slot.

Keep the follow-up connected

Use your workspace to review assigned leads, keep notes, prepare client conversations, and organize next steps with your team. Timing and volume depend on opportunities becoming available; there is no guaranteed daily supply or closing.
