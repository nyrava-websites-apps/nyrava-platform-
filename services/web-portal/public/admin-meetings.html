Availability

This stays on until you turn it off. There is no 30-minute activation window. Turning it off ends access checks for current calls.

Meeting length

Up to 12 hours per meeting. Realtors can extend their team meetings within this maximum. Calendar client invitations keep their existing appointment duration rules.

Current capacity

One simultaneous meeting, two participants. These limits remain until larger real calls and server load have been tested. Recording is off.
