Basic: up to 5 distinct ZIPs. Pro: up to 20. Maximum 2 subscriber businesses per ZIP.

What to expect after adding ZIP codes

Allow approximately 3–5 days for qualified leads to begin arriving. This is an expected timeframe, not a guaranteed delivery date. Leads arrive as qualified opportunities become available, not daily.

Basic: maximum 2 leads per business day and 10 per rolling 30 days. Pro: maximum 3 per business day and 20 per rolling 30 days. These are delivery ceilings, not guaranteed quantities. Daily limits reset at midnight America/New_York.

Predictive analytics help identify possible buying or selling interest, while our website valuation and buyer/seller tools collect consumer inquiries. A valuation request or prediction alone is not a qualified lead. Before delivery we require a name, location, at least one verified email or phone channel, current buyer/seller intent and permission for the designated Realtor. AI screens evidence; flagged records receive human review. Each delivered lead goes exclusively to one subscriber business. Appointments and transactions are not guaranteed.
