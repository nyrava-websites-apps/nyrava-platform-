RELATIONSHIPS YOU ALREADY BUILT

Your email list belongs in your business workflow.

New platform opportunities are one part of your pipeline. Past clients, referrals, buyers still exploring, and homeowners you already know also deserve organized follow-up.

Keep your own contacts in your CRM with their names, email addresses, phone numbers, ZIP codes, buying or selling interests, and relevant notes. Assign responsibility within your team so a useful relationship does not depend on remembering a separate spreadsheet or message.

A contact is more than an email address.

Record what they need, what you discussed, and what should happen next. Build continuity around the person instead of starting over at every conversation.

Use contact information within the permissions provided by that person. Saving a contact does not automatically subscribe them to bulk marketing or establish permission for every type of outreach.

CLEAR RESPONSIBILITY

Assign the lead. Keep the oversight.

Review opportunities in your workspace and choose whether to handle them yourself or delegate follow-up to a team member. Change an assignment when availability or responsibilities change.

  1. Review the opportunity. Understand the inquiry, available contact details, location, and current status.
  2. Choose who follows up. Keep responsibility with you or assign the work to an authorized team member.
  3. Keep the history connected. Record the conversation, update progress, and plan the next action.
  4. Adjust when needed. Reassign the work while keeping it connected to your business.

Team delegation does not transfer your subscriber relationship, create another subscription, or reserve additional ZIP slots. Plan limits and contact restrictions still apply.

SUPPORT BESIDE THE WORK
