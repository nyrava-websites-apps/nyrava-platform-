Collection controls

One shared scan per active ZIP, up to two ZIPs every 15 minutes. Each ZIP is checked at most every six hours. Maximum 20 ZIP searches per UTC day and three property records per ZIP scan. Access blocks pause work; no bypass is attempted.

Supported source: forsalebyowner.com. Its MIT extraction library does not grant rights to collect from the source website. Enable only after recording an authorized collection basis. This source uses the public listing-data endpoint used by its website; no extra search or AI key is required. Source access and availability can change.

Three-day collection pilot

Run two temporary ZIPs for internal evaluation. Pilot ZIPs expire automatically after 72 hours, share the daily scan allowance, and reserve no Realtor territory. Results remain in admin review.

Maximum two pilot ZIPs. You can stop either early.
